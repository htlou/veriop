# Four sampled llama.cpp commits with missing local source blobs

The local [changed-blob audit](../../results/pinned-changed-blob-availability.json) reports `missing_changed_blob` for all four commits below. Their exact upstream commit pages expose the changed paths and historical diff for screening. This does **not** restore the complete old/new source trees or provide runnable checker inputs.

| Commit | Historical change and optimization intent | Fixed-sample checker result |
|---|---|---|

These classifications are checker-scope findings based on the upstream historical diffs, not an executed equivalence/refinement proof or counterexample. No local official before/after benchmark was run for these four commits; Kuiper did not check them.
